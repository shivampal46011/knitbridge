Transparency

Our promises, in plain words

What we pay for if we get it wrong, what we will never do, and who we actually are.

If we get it wrong, we pay

  • Our spec was wrong? If a mistake in the spec we wrote means a re-sample, we pay for it.
  • Our quote missed a cost? We cover the gap, up to our fee on that order.
  • The mill lets you down? We move your order to another vetted mill, free, and the failing mill leaves our network.

We will never

  • Hold your money. You always pay the mill directly, against its GST invoice.
  • Add our own markup to the mill's price.
  • Share your designs, prices or contact with anyone without your OK.
  • Charge buyers a fee. The sample fee goes to the mill, not to us.

Who we are

  • We're new. KnitBridge started in October 2026, in India.
  • Our mill network is small and growing. We add a mill only after checking its GST, a factory video call and its certificates.
  • Business registration (Udyam/GST) is in progress. Numbers will be listed here once issued.
  • We'd rather tell you this than show you made-up numbers.

GSM = grams per square metre, so higher means thicker and warmer. Not sure? Send a photo of a garment you like and we'll match it.

Rotary screen

How: an engraved drum per colour rolls the print onto moving fabric.

Best for: bigger runs of the same design. One-time screen cost, then cheapest per kg. Screens kept for repeats.

Digital print

How: a print head sprays tiny ink drops, like an office printer for fabric.

Best for: small lots, many colours, photo-like art. No screen cost, higher per kg.

Screen / DTF on garments

How: ink pushed through a mesh stencil (screen), or a printed film heat-pressed on (DTF).

Best for: logos and 1–4 colour art on finished tees and hoodies. Usually included in the piece price.

For 40–50 kg per design, digital often works out cheaper than rotary once screen charges are counted. We quote both so you can compare.

Questions

Good to know

Who pays you?

The mill pays us a 5% introduction fee on orders they get paid for. Quotes and sourcing are free for buyers.

Do you hold my money?

No. You pay the mill directly, against their GST invoice, in stages: advance after sample approval, balance after the pre-dispatch check.

What's the minimum order?

Usually 50 kg per colour for custom-dyed fabric and 50–100 pieces per style for garments. Shade pools will bring fabric down to 20–30 kg on shared shades.

What if bulk doesn't match the sample?

Every order has a signed spec sheet with tolerances and your approved lab dip or strike-off. If bulk falls outside it, the mill fixes or replaces it, and we mediate within 48 hours.

Can you ship outside India?

Yes. For garment orders we can quote with export freight. Mention it in the form.
