Privacy notice

Last updated 23 October 2026 · Written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
In short: we collect only what we need to get you quotes and manage your order. We never sell it. Your phone number never goes to a mill without your OK. Ask us anytime to see or delete your data.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhy
Name, business name, WhatsApp number, email (optional), cityTo reply to you, send quotes and manage your order
Your requirement: product, specs, quantities, artwork, target priceTo prepare a spec and get quotes from mills
GST number or store link (optional)To prioritise serious orders and confirm a business is real
For mills: company, contact person, GSTIN, capabilitiesTo verify the mill and match it with suitable buyers
Your IP address when you submit a formOnly to block spam and abuse

We use no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies.

Who sees it

  • Mills see your spec, quantities and artwork so they can quote. They see your name and contact only after you approve that mill.
  • Buyers see a mill's name, quote and capabilities when we send quotes.
  • No one else. We do not sell or rent data, ever.

Where it's kept

Form submissions are stored on our own computer in India, not with a third-party CRM. Messages you send us on WhatsApp or email are also held by those services under their own policies. The website pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; the forms send data straight to our computer.

How long we keep it

Up to 2 years after our last contact with you, or less if you ask. We may keep order records longer where tax law requires (for example, invoices).

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Message us and we'll act within 7 days. If you're unhappy with how we handled it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
