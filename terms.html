Terms

Last updated 23 October 2026 · Written in plain English. If anything is unclear, ask us.
The short version
  • We connect buyers with knit mills in Tiruppur and manage the order between them. We are not the seller.
  • Buyers pay the mill directly. We never hold anyone's money.
  • The mill pays us 5% on orders it gets paid for. Buyers pay us nothing.
  • If we make a mistake, we fix it at our cost, up to our fee on that order.

1. Who we are

KnitBridge is a sourcing service based in India. We started in October 2026. Our business registration (Udyam/GST) is in progress, and we will publish the numbers here once they are issued.

2. What we do

We help buyers (brands, traders, businesses) source knit fabric and garments from mills in Tiruppur. We turn your requirement into a written spec, collect quotes from mills we have checked, arrange samples, follow up on production, coordinate a check before dispatch, and help sort out problems.

3. What we are not

We do not buy, sell, own or store goods. The sale is a contract directly between the buyer and the mill. The mill invoices the buyer, the buyer pays the mill, and the mill delivers to the buyer. We act as an introducer and coordinator.

4. How we are paid

The mill pays us a fee of 5% of the invoice value (excluding GST) on orders from buyers we introduce, including repeat orders for 24 months. The fee is due only after the mill is paid. Buyers pay us nothing. Sample or lab-dip fees are paid to the mill. We do not add any markup to the mill's price.

5. The spec sheet

Before bulk production, the buyer and the mill both approve a written spec sheet. It lists the fabric, GSM and allowed variation, colours (matched to the approved lab dip or strike-off), print details, quantity, price, payment stages, delivery date, and how inspection and rejection work. The spec sheet is the agreement on quality. Once approved, changes are re-quoted.

6. Payments

  • Buyers pay the mill directly, by bank transfer or UPI, against the mill's GST proforma or tax invoice.
  • Typical stages: sample fee → advance after sample approval → balance after the pre-dispatch check, before dispatch. The exact stages are written on each spec sheet.
  • For large first orders we may suggest a licensed escrow service. We never collect buyer payments into our own account.
  • After sample approval, the sample fee and advance are generally non-refundable, because custom dyeing and printing cannot be resold.

7. Our promises (and their limits)

  • If a mistake in a spec we wrote causes a re-sample, we pay for that re-sample.
  • If our landed quote missed a cost, we cover the difference, up to our fee on that order.
  • If a mill fails to deliver as agreed, we help move the order to another mill in our network, free of charge.
  • We mediate any dispute between buyer and mill within 48 hours of being told, using the signed spec sheet.

Our total liability for any order is limited to the fee we earn on that order. Responsibility for the goods themselves (quality, quantity, delivery) lies with the mill under the spec sheet. Mills in our network agree in writing to take responsibility for defects in what they make.

8. What buyers agree to

  • Give accurate requirements, and approve or reject samples in good time.
  • Own, or have permission to use, any artwork, logos or prints you send us.
  • Pay the mill as per the spec sheet. Mills registered as MSMEs are entitled to payment within agreed terms (no more than 45 days) under the MSMED Act, 2006.
  • Not use our introductions to deal with the mill behind our back.

9. What mills agree to

Mills sign our separate mill agreement. In short: quote honestly, keep buyer designs confidential and never resell them, produce to the signed spec, share lab dips before bulk, share invoice copies with us, take responsibility for defects, and not bypass us with buyers we introduce.

10. Confidentiality

We share your spec, quantities and artwork with mills only so they can quote and produce. We do not share your contact details with a mill until you approve that mill. See our Privacy notice.

11. If something goes wrong

  1. Tell us through the contact form on our website or the channel you use with your order contact. We respond within 24 hours and mediate within 48 hours.
  2. If buyer and mill still disagree, either can refer the matter to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Bengaluru, in English.
  3. These terms are governed by the laws of India. Courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka have jurisdiction.

12. Agreeing electronically

Approvals and acceptances sent by WhatsApp or email (for example "Agreed" or "Approved") are binding, the same as a signature.

13. Changes

We may update these terms as we grow. The date at the top will change, and orders already confirmed follow the terms that applied when they were confirmed.
