Questions

Good to know

Who pays you?

The mill pays us a 5% introduction fee on orders they get paid for. Quotes and sourcing are free for buyers.

Do you hold my money?

No. You pay the mill directly, against their GST invoice, in stages: advance after sample approval, balance after the pre-dispatch check.

What's the minimum order?

Usually 50 kg per colour for custom-dyed fabric and 50–100 pieces per style for garments. Shade pools will bring fabric down to 20–30 kg on shared shades.

What if bulk doesn't match the sample?

Every order has a signed spec sheet with tolerances and your approved lab dip or strike-off. If bulk falls outside it, the mill fixes or replaces it, and we mediate within 48 hours.

Can you ship outside India?

Yes. For garment orders we can quote with export freight. Mention it in the form.
